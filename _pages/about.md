Ananda A. Squire is a doctoral student in Public Affairs at Florida International Univeristy, specilaizing in urban policy, development and resilience. Her research interests sit at the intersection of collaborative governance, resilience planning and building scalable urban infrastructure. Currently, in her dissertation, she uses GIS, causal inference, and empirical policy evaluation to analyze how municipal institutions prepare critical urban infrastructure and health systems for extreme climate events. She holds a B.Sc. in Political Science and International Relations with First Class Honors from the University of the West Indies, Mona, where she also received the Nethersole Prize for graduating with the highest GPA in the Political Science program.
